# Public information on request

**Initialization status: Not adopted.** This optional policy is outside the
active register. Installing the plugin, initializing a city, or adopting the
unmodified default charter does not appoint the Public Information Officer.
To opt in, review and copy this policy to `.city/policies/public-information.md`,
add the appointment below to the charter, and record the combined revision
with `adopt` or `amend`. Do not change the default pilot roster.

```markdown
### Additional appointment: Public Information Officer

On adoption of this charter and the public-information policy, appoint the
bundled `public-information-officer.agent.md` to scope resident requests and
prepare specifically authorized information products. The appointment has
no standing assignment, approval authority, or publication authority.
```

## Mandate and authority

Resident Services receives actual resident requests; City Manager coordinates
scoping, authorization, review, and handoff. The Officer may clarify an
existing request without drafting the product, and prepares the product only
on a scoped Manager assignment after a recorded case-specific decision. A
routine documentation class does not substitute for this decision. No office
originates a product, channel, or schedule under this policy.

- **Repository change:** Use the normal work order, project scope, and applied
  `authorize` decision. The Council acts only within its adopted delegation;
  send reserved matters to the resident. The Officer contributes editorial work
  without substituting for Engineering delivery, independent inspection, or
  resident acceptance. Do not label an editorial assignment as approval to edit
  the repository.
- **Standalone product:** Resident Services prepares a minimized intake brief
  and routes it through the Manager for Records Officer capture as an
  `information-case` artifact. Its initial `ART-` ID is the case reference;
  use no work-order or fictitious project link. The Clerk documents the
  authorized resident's actual decision on the exact brief (case and revision,
  decider role, date, outcome, scope, and any available context). Route it
  through the Manager for filing as an attributed case revision. No agent can
  substitute its own decision. Stop if the decision is missing,
  contested, stale, or withdrawn; do not seek identity proof. Record acceptance separately against the
  reviewed product's exact revision. Do not use `complete` for this route.

Source offices check factual claims, the Clerk checks any official decision
citations, and the Records Officer checks classification and custody. Return
the reviewed revision to the Manager for acceptance and authorized handoff.
Keep restricted copy in a suitably controlled location and record only safe
references in Git. Append case and content revisions; pause on conflicting
records, a changed audience, or material changes to approved scope.

Case notes preserve provenance, not runtime approval or authenticated identity.
An artifact ID, filing category, or `--apply` is not consent. Keep existing
repository permissions and review controls in place.

## External release and corrections

External publication is not part of the office's assignment, even for
public-facing content. If release is requested, the resident decides separately
on the exact accepted revision, audience, and destination, and arranges any
release outside the office. No product is posted automatically. Route requests
for corrections through Resident Services and link the revised product to the
earlier version without silently overwriting it. Escalate disputed approval
or disclosure through the existing Auditor and resident route.
