# Plan Checking

Under adopted [development applications](../city-development-applications/SKILL.md),
Chief Planner independently assesses the exact current Advisor application revision
against proportionate screening requirements and resident answers. An Inspector
desk check cannot replace that assessment, nor can Advisor assess its own packet.
Do not require every artifact kind, master plan or full impact matrix by default.

## Competency and application

Municipal plan checking is a desk assessment against applicable requirements,
not construction or permission to proceed. In Repository City, evaluate whether
a proposal is coherent and reviewable without rewriting it into compliance.

## Triggers and inputs

- Use before resident consideration, after a material proposal revision, or when
  acceptance criteria and planned work disagree.
- Require root, case, exact proposal revision, criteria, applicable standards,
  architecture constraints, district ownership, and claimed approval evidence.
- Read original source requirements. Record absent or disputed baselines explicitly.

## Procedure

1. Establish review type and scope. A plan check is a proposal desk review for
   the City Council or resident, not a delivery inspection or permit.
2. List each applicable requirement with its exact source and current status:
   established requirement, resident proposal, disputed, or unknown.
3. Check that the plan maps outcomes to bounded edits, interfaces, dependencies,
   observable acceptance evidence, and a credible recovery approach.
4. Examine omitted error paths, operational impacts, ownership conflicts,
   compatibility, and missing specialist assessments.
5. Check consistency between scope, proposed tests, and affected districts.
   Do not expand or weaken criteria to make the plan pass.
6. Separate a clear nonconformity from insufficient evidence and an advisory
   improvement. State precisely what would resolve each issue.
7. Compare revisions when available, retaining unresolved findings and identifying
   new impacts rather than assuming a revised document fixed everything.
8. Return findings to the Clerk for recording and the resident for consideration.
   The inspector does not repair the plan or approve the author's work.

## Structured result and evidence

Return root/case/revision, review scope, requirement-to-plan matrix, evidence
locations, findings, missing prerequisites, and `pass`, `fail`, or `blocked`
per item and overall.
A `pass` means the scoped proposal is ready for resident consideration only.
Provide precise file/section references and distinguish reviewed from unreviewed areas.

## Escalation and limits

- A skill grants no edit or execution powers. Building Inspector never edits
  requirements, tests, proposals, source, records, or enforcement controls.
- Disputed standards or missing resident choices go to the resident through the
  Clerk/Manager; do not assume specialist clearance or fake approval.
- Local files and write confirmation are not authorization. A desk-review pass
  informs the Council's decision; it does not authorize delivery itself.
